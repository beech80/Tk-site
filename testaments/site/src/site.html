The experience

Three ways of telling one story.

I

Photography

Portraits made where a life actually happens: on the street, under the trees, against a wall in the last of the light. The photograph is another language for the story.

II

Conversation

Unscripted interviews, sometimes walking, sometimes sitting still. Answers are kept in the subject’s own words, with the pauses left in.

III

Reflection

Dialogue and images are composed into a chapter of about twenty-four pages: a record to read slowly and come back to.

About TESTAMENTS

THE STORY BEHIND THE STORY.

Most of us encounter people in fragments. A photograph. A conversation. A first impression. A moment in time.

But a person is more than the moment in which we meet them.

There are beginnings we never witnessed, struggles we never saw, decisions we never understood, and dreams we may never have imagined.

TESTAMENTS was created to explore what exists beneath the surface.

The mission

To preserve personal stories through meaningful conversations, cinematic photography, and thoughtful editorial storytelling.

The vision

To build a lasting collection of human experiences that encourages understanding, reflection, and connection across different walks of life.

Our approach

Conversation, not a script.

ListeningObservingPhotographingDocumentingShaping

TESTAMENTS is built around genuine conversation rather than scripted answers. We listen first, and we let the subject lead us to what matters.

The editorial process moves from listening to observing, photographing and documenting, and then to shaping everything into a chapter that reads as one piece.

Photography is not decoration. It is another language through which the subject’s story is told. The goal is to photograph people honestly, in environments and moments that feel true to them.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What is TESTAMENTS?

An independent magazine that tells the stories behind people through personal interviews, intimate conversations and cinematic photography. Each chapter focuses on one person, or occasionally two.

Who can be featured?

Anyone. A person’s place in TESTAMENTS isn’t decided by wealth, popularity, status or following. Ordinary people have extraordinary stories too.

Does it cost anything to be featured?

No. Interviews and photography for featured subjects are free.

When does TESTAMENTS launch?

How often are new chapters released?

The plan is one new chapter each month.

Can I buy a printed copy?

Printed editions of about twenty-four pages are planned in small runs. They aren’t on sale yet; prices and availability will be announced when each edition opens. Join the newsletter to hear first.

Will copies be sold in shops?

Selected local businesses are being considered as stockists. Locations will be listed on the Stockists page once they are confirmed.

Privacy

Draft for review before launch

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us: your email address if you subscribe to the newsletter; the details you enter in the contact form; and, once the shop opens, what’s needed to fulfil an order, such as your name and shipping address.

How we use it

To reply to you, to review story submissions, to send the newsletter you asked for, and to deliver orders. We don’t sell your information.

Payments

Payments will be handled by a third-party payment provider. TESTAMENTS will never see or store your full card details.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in every email, and you can ask us to see or delete the information we hold about you by using the contact page.

Accessibility

Our aim

TESTAMENTS aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. Every photograph carries a written description, the whole site can be used with a keyboard, focus is always visible, and motion is reduced when your device asks for it.

The reader

The digital reader can be turned with the arrow keys, by swiping, or with the on-screen buttons, and can be zoomed. As chapters are published we’ll also offer a text version you can select and read aloud.
