02 · About me

Hi, I’m Quadia.

I’m a twenty-something woman of faith learning, out loud, what it means to become who God created me to be. Talking Through Twenties is where I share that journey: the prayers, the routines, the wins and the questions.

Say hello → Read along

My story

Like most women in this decade, I’ve had seasons of certainty and seasons of starting over. Somewhere between building a career and building a life, I realised how many of us are carrying the same questions quietly.

So I started saying them out loud. What began as honest posts became a community of women who want their faith, their femininity and their future to fit together.

My foundation

I’m a Christian first. My faith is not a category on this site; it is the lens for all of it. I believe God is intentional about every season, including the confusing ones, and I want every conversation here to point back to Him.

My work

I bring a professional, detail-minded approach to everything I create: thoughtful planning, clear communication and a polished finish. Brands and creatives who work with me get a reliable partner as well as a beautiful result.

What I love

Morning devotionalsFine jewelrySkincare ritualsTravel diariesJournalingNeutral wardrobesWorship playlistsSlow Sundays

Why Talking Through Twenties

Because this decade asks a lot of us, and it goes better with company. I wanted a place that feels like a conversation with a close friend over coffee: soft, honest, faith-filled and full of hope for who you’re becoming.

What I want you to know

You are not behind. You are being built.

with love, Quadia
